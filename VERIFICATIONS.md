# Verifications of Professional Men's Basketball League Classifications

This document provides the information data sources and verbatim excerpts that substantiate the classifications recorded in the `Classification of Professional Men's Basketball Leagues` table in `README.md`.

Each entry evaluates the league against the four defined criteria:
1. **Criteria 1 (Governing Authority)**: A governing authority oversees the league.
2. **Criteria 2 (Incorporated Teams)**: The league's teams operate as incorporated entities.
3. **Criteria 3 (Primary Employment)**: Players are employed primarily as basketball players.
4. **Criteria 4 (Recurring Schedule)**: The league has a recurring schedule.
