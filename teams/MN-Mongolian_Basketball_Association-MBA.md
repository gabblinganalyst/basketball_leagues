## Introduction

This file lists the teams in the Mongolia Mongolian Basketball Association. This directory is limited to metadata. Each piece of information was verified by a person-in-the-loop. Despite this verification of metadata, the information on record may need to be updated over time, and for more details please take a look at the Appendix's [List of Reasons for Stale URLs](#list-of-reasons-for-stale-urls). In addition, the file is expected to grow over time based on on the following dimensions:
1. The number of teams covered (due to league contraction/expansion)
2. The number of languages supported
3. The type of metadata covered
